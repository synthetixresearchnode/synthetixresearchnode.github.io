Privacy Policy

Naval Warfare C4I — Android game by Synthetix Research Node

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the mobile game Naval Warfare C4I (the “Game”, Android package io.github.synthetixresearchnode.navalwarfare) and the third-party services built into it handle, and what choices you have. The Game is developed by Synthetix Research Node (“we”, “us”).

1. In short

  • The Game has no user accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. We do not receive, store, sell or share your personal data.
  • Your progress, settings and statistics are stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects certain data from your device to deliver ads (see section 3).
  • The optional “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Google Play.

2. Data stored on your device

The Game stores your progress, settings, statistics and the status of the “Remove Ads” purchase locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data in your device settings.

3. Advertising: Google AdMob

The Game shows full-screen ads after battles (not during the tutorial). Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. According to Google, the SDK automatically collects the following data and shares it with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the approximate location of the device;
  • app interactions: app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic data: app launch time, hang rate and energy usage;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

This data is encrypted in transit (TLS). We do not receive it in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated ad reports. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game shows Google’s consent form before ads are requested. You can allow or refuse personalized ads and the related use of your data, and change your choice at any time through the privacy settings option in the Game’s settings menu.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Once the ID is deleted, apps can no longer use it for personalized ads.
  • You can manage how Google personalizes ads in My Ad Center.
  • The “Remove Ads” purchase turns off ads in the Game.

5. In-app purchase: Google Play

“Remove Ads” is a one-time purchase sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we do not receive your name, card details or billing information. Google Play tells the Game whether the purchase has been made, so that the Game can turn off ads and restore the purchase after reinstallation. Google Play’s processing of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not collect personal data ourselves. If you believe that a child has provided personal data through the Game, contact us and we will help you resolve it with Google.

7. Retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data on your device is deleted as described in section 2. Data collected by Google is retained under Google’s policies; you can control it with the options in section 4 and your Google Account.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/EEA and the UK, Brazil’s LGPD, or the privacy laws of certain US states), you may have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of personalized advertising. Because the Game itself does not collect personal data, requests about advertising data are handled by Google; the options in section 4 let you make the main choices directly. For any question, contact us — we will reply and help.

9. Changes

We will publish any changes on this page and update the effective date. If we add features that handle data differently (for example, Google Play Games achievements), we will update this policy before releasing them.

10. Contact

Synthetix Research Node — synthetixresearchnode@gmail.com — Israel.
